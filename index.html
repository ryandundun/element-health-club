Our philosophy

Four elements. One complete practice.

Real wellbeing is not one thing. It is contrast, movement, recovery and nourishment working together. We built the club so you can touch all four in a single visit.

Water

Shock the system, calm the mind. Cold immersion and hydration that resets you from the inside.

  • Cold plunge
  • Juice & protein bar
  • Smoothie café

Fire

Heat, light and intensity. Sweat it out, recover faster and build real conditioning.

  • Sauna
  • Red light therapy
  • HIIT

Earth

Strength, stability and control. The foundation that everything else is built on.

  • Fitness classes

A day at Element

Come for an hour. Leave feeling like a new person.

There is no right way to use the club, but here is one we love. Mix it up however your body asks.

Plan your first visit
  1. 07:00

    Move

    Start with HIIT, pilates or a trampoline class. Wake the body up.

  2. 07:50

    Heat

    Fifteen minutes in the sauna to loosen everything.

  3. 08:10

    Cold

    Three minutes in the plunge. Breathe. This is the part you will talk about.

  4. 08:20

    Light

    A red light session while your heart rate settles.

  5. 08:40

    Refuel

    Protein shake or a smoothie bowl at the café. Then go run your day.

  6. 19:00

    Dance

    Come back for salsa. Because you are in Medellín.
