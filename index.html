Questions

Good to know before your first class.

How do I book my free first class?

Pick a class on the Classes page and book online. Your first class is free, Recovery included. One per person.

What is Recovery?

Our sauna and cold plunge area. After every class you can stay for a Recovery session of up to an hour, included. It is optional: stay ten minutes, the full hour or skip it.

How much does it cost after my first class?

A day pass is $60,000 COP, or try 3 classes in 7 days for $100,000 COP. Founding memberships start at $349,000 COP a month. See memberships.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. There is no contract, so you can cancel anytime, and you can freeze it for up to 30 days a year.

Where are you and when are you open?

Tv 39D #74-105, 4th floor, Laureles - Estadio, Medellín. Monday to Friday 6:00 to 21:00, Saturday 8:00 to 20:00, Sunday closed. See the map.
